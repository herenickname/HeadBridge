# Community guidelines

Be respectful, specific, and patient. HeadBridge contributors test different
hardware, firmware, languages, and levels of protocol knowledge; disagreement
about a packet or design is not a reason to attack a person.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and publishing
another person's device identifiers or private correspondence are not accepted.
Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation to protect the
project and its contributors.

Report conduct concerns privately through the repository's Security reporting
channel. Good-faith technical criticism and reverse-engineering discussion are
welcome when they follow the project's clean-room and privacy rules.
